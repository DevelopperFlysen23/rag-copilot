Rockwell Automation PowerFlex 525 - Fault F007 (Bus Undervoltage)
Symptom: Drive faults on bus undervoltage during deceleration.
Checks:
- Measure the DC bus at the terminals and inspect the DC link capacitors.
- Enable flux braking / DC injection (P37).
- Verify supply phase imbalance is below 3 percent.
- Inspect the pre-charge relay.
Recovery: add a line reactor, enable the braking resistor, and clear the fault with Reset.
