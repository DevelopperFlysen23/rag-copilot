Schneider Electric M340 - Unity Pro / Modbus TCP comms loss
Symptom: PLC drops Modbus TCP sessions to the SCADA host.
Checks:
- Confirm CPU ETH port link and check for an IP conflict on the VLAN.
- Verify the %MW addressing range matches the HMI tag table.
- Increase the watchdog (IO scanner timeout) to 500 ms.
- Check the switch spanning-tree state for a blocking port.
Recovery: rebuild the IO scanner, ping the gateway, and reboot the NOC switch if CRC errors climb.
