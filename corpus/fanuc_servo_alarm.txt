Fanuc Servo Alarm 4xx - OVC (Overcurrent)
Symptom: SV0401 alarm, servo amplifier overcurrent.
Checks:
- Measure insulation resistance of the motor windings (>10 M ohm).
- Verify servo parameter No.2020 (motor ID) is correct.
- Check for mechanical binding on the axis.
- Inspect the amplifier module for a failed IGBT.
Recovery: power cycle, run manual feed with reduced gain, and replace the amplifier if the fault persists.
